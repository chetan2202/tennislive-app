Serving

You bounce the ball while you aim with the arrows. Space tosses. Hit just before the ball reaches the ring: Space for your default serve, or I kick · L flat · K slice · J topspin. A second serve without a pick is a safe kick.

Controller

Left stick: move/aim · A/✕ default shot · Y/△ up · B/○ right · X/□ down · LB/L1 left (shots as for I L K J) · RT/R2 flat · Start pause

Touch

Drag the left side to move. HIT is a joystick: tap the middle for your default shot, or press or slide toward an edge to pick the shot written there. LOB to lob.

Practice

Pick Practice and a drill. Each drill is 20 balls: 3 points in a yellow target, 1 point in the court. Your best score is saved per drill and coach level.

Exit

Hold the × button at the top (or hold Esc) to leave a match or drill.
