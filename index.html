Serving

Arrows move the yellow target. Space tosses the ball. Press Space again just before the ball reaches the ring above you — the ring turns yellow in the sweet spot. Double-tap for a harder serve.

Controller

Left stick: move/aim · A/✕ hit · X/□ drop · B/○ deep · Y/△ lob · RT/R2 power · Start pause

Touch

Drag the left side to move. HIT to swing, POWER for a power shot, LOB to lob.
